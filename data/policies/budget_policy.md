# Budget and Approval Policy (Nimbus Holdings)

*Written for this project as a realistic example. Not a real company's policy.*

## Monthly team budgets
Budgets are set once a year from each team's typical month plus 15% headroom. They cover effective cost
(after discounts and credits) across all providers, cloud and AI.

## Alerts and escalation
- At **80%** of the monthly budget, the team lead receives an alert.
- At **100%**, the team lead must explain the overrun to the FinOps lead within 3 working days.
- An overrun above **120%** for two months in a row triggers a budget review with the CFO's office.

## Approvals for new spend
- New recurring spend above **$2,000 per month** needs FinOps approval before launch.
- One-time purchases above **$10,000** (support plans, licences, prepayments) need CFO approval and must
  be logged in the contracts calendar.
- One-time purchases are excluded from anomaly alerts but count against the shared budget.

## Forecasting
Teams forecast the next quarter by the 20th of the last month of each quarter. A launch that is expected
to change spend by more than 10% must be included in the forecast.
